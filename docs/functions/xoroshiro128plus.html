      Creates a xoroshiro128+ pseudo-random number generator

      Xoroshiro128+ is a fast, high-quality PRNG with excellent statistical properties. It maintains 128 bits of state and has a period of 2^128 - 1. Perfect for games and simulations requiring deterministic, seedable randomness.
